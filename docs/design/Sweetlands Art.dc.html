Sweetlands Imperium · art from the rulebook
The five factions and their treats
The last five pages of the rulebook carry one portrait per faction and two small treat icons under each: the faction's own treat, which is a fixed tile on the board in its region, and one of the four random treats, paired with that leader for flavor only. The random treats are shuffled at the start of each game. These are the real graphics for the game. Where the table and the setup screen use colored circles with an initial today, these go in their place: the portrait as the seat's avatar and on the kingdom picker, the treat icons on the treat tiles and on treat Intel cards.

Where the portraits go
The seat avatar on the table and in the bench column, the kingdom picker at table setup, the lobby seat, and the end-of-game results. Crop to a circle from the face; the full portrait shows on the game page and the kingdom picker.
Where the treat icons go
The treat tiles on the board (today a text label), the chip on a treat Intel card (today a dashed circle with a question mark), and the log line when a treat is played. They are single-color glyphs, so they take the ink color and work in both themes.
The random treats are random
The second icon under each leader is flavor: the four random treats (ice cream cone, chocolate bar, gummy bear, cupcake) are shuffled at the start of every game and one lands in each region's random-treat tile. So the board can show any of the four in any region, and the game start is a moment to animate: four tiles flip to reveal which treat landed where.
