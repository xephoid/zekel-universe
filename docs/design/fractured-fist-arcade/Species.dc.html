THE FOUR SPECIES · OUTLINES TO SCALE (HUMAN = 6 FT)
Human
Native to this world. The baseline everyone else is measured against.
    Grankiki
    Insectoid, about four feet tall, with a chameleon’s head and eyes that move on their own. Seldom seen alone.
    • CHOICEFour arms, a big pair and a small pair, for the insect and for flurries.
    • CHOICEA curled chameleon tail for balance.
    Bouaux
    Bird-like and human-sized in their first century, able to glide. Shown here in that young phase: the one that fights.
    • CHOICEHuge gorilla arms, dragged on the knuckles when they walk.
    • CHOICEBat wings: skin stretched from arm to leg, with a spur off each elbow. Folded at rest, spread to glide.
    • CHOICEA long neck and crest that hint at the elder form.
    Unmoored
    Present but hard to describe; the form slips from memory as you look. Intent arrives as vibes, not words.
    • CHOICEEach opponent sees its own kind: the Unmoored wears the outline of whoever it fights. Shown here as a human, with the other shapes it could take behind it.
    • CHOICEDrawn faceless, with a broken edge, out of step with itself, pieces drifting off.
    • CHOICEThe vibe rings are how they “speak”.
    Bouaux, elder phase
    After their first century: brontosaurus-sized, flightless, settled. A human for scale. Not a fighter, so no poses.
