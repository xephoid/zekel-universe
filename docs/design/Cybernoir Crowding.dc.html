One rule underneath all of them: a count is never a substitute for the cards when the player is entitled to see them. A pile the Hacker may browse opens on a tap; a pile nobody may see shows its count and nothing else. The current table gets this backwards in one place — it draws the Detective's own face-down informants as card backs to the Detective.
