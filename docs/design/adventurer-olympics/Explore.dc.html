Exploring: pick a slot, turn the tile
Tiles always sit in a fixed pattern, so the player never drags a tile freely. They pick one of the glowing slots, then turn the tile through its six ways. Both are the player's choices; the server only checks them.

Dungeon Map card, and forced explore
Same steps, but every empty slot touching the map glows, not only the ones next to you. A forced explore happens at the start of a turn when every icon on the map is done; then the turn carries on.
When Explore is greyed out, say why
"You're on a centre hex; centre hexes touch no slot." · "No steps left." · "Both slots next to you are taken." · "No tiles left." The button stays put so players learn where it lives.
The last tile placed starts the end: finish the round, play one more, then score.
