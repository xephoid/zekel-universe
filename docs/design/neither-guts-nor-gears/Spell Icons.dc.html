Spells — eleven things a wizard casts Five of these already had a mark on the research sheet, and they keep it exactly: a spell and the research that unlocks it are the same thing seen at two moments, so drawing them twice would teach a player that they were different. Six are new, and they are the ones that arrive with a unit or a hero rather than being bought.
5 REUSED 6 NEW
Wizard Missile 1 MANA · EVOKER
NEW · COMES WITH THE UNIT A bolt with speed lines behind it. The cheapest thing in the game and the one cast most often, so it is the plainest mark on the sheet.
Ice Blast 3 MANA · EVOKER
SAME MARK AS THE RESEARCH Drops one unit 100 initiative. The arm that points down is the whole message, and it is what tells this apart from Fiery Explosion at a glance.
Fiery Explosion 3 MANA · EVOKER
SAME MARK AS THE RESEARCH Two damage to an area. Rays going every way, because nothing about it has a direction — the deliberate opposite of Ice Blast.
Mana Shield 1 MANA A POINT · ANY CASTER
SAME MARK AS THE RESEARCH Evokers, Enchantresses and Clerics all buy damage off with it. The only spell on the sheet with three casters, and its cost is open-ended.
Enhance 2 MANA · ENCHANTRESS
NEW · COMES WITH THE UNIT Two chevrons rising, and no body. That is the rule this sheet adds: a mark with a body is a unit, the effect alone is the spell.
Dehance 2 MANA · ENCHANTRESS
REDRAWN · BODY DROPPED The same chevrons falling. Takes 1 defense off a unit. Its research card on the other sheet was carrying a body it should not have had.
Detection FREE · ENCHANTRESS
SAME MARK AS THE RESEARCH The only spell here that costs no mana, which is why it gets a button on the battle screen rather than a place in the casting list.
[CLERIC’S SAVE] 5 MANA · CLERIC · ONCE A PASS
NEW · NAME NEEDED A dome over a standing figure. The faction board describes the effect but never names it, so the mark is drawn and the label is a slot.
Decoy ON REVEAL · SPY HERO
NEW · COMES WITH THE SPY A dashed figure left behind and a solid one stepped aside. The attack does nothing and she moves alone, never back the way it came.
[ELARA’S REACH] 5 MANA · ARCHMAGE ELARA · NOT A BATTLE
NEW · NAME NEEDED Two hexes and a bolt across the seam. The only mark in the set with a hexagon in it, because it is the only thing a wizard casts that crosses a tile boundary.
Invisible ON REVEAL · SPY HERO
SAME MARK AS THE RESEARCH Kills one opposing hero outright. Fully dashed, and Decoy is half dashed, so the two spy powers read as a pair with a difference.
A spell and its research are one mark, not two. Ice Blast on the Research action, Ice Blast on the technology track, Ice Blast in the casting list during a battle, Ice Blast in the log afterwards — one drawing in all four places. Six of the eleven spells here are never bought at all: they arrive with the Evoker, the Enchantress, the Cleric, the spy or Elara herself, so they appear on this sheet and nowhere on the research one. That difference is the only thing the two sheets disagree about, and it is a real difference in the rules.
Two things need a decision Neither the Cleric’s five-mana save nor Archmage Elara’s spell has a name in the design document. Both need one before they can appear in a log line or a numbered voice list, because both have to say something out loud.
