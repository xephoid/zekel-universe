Robot units — twelve marks Twelve against the wizards’ seven, and five of the twelve are collectors that differ only in what they take. Those five share one frame with a resource glyph inside, so the family reads as a family and the difference sits where the difference is.
12 OF 12 5 SHARE A FRAME
Core POWER · 1 WATER
A cell in its housing. Every collector and platform below is drawn assuming one of these is inside it; a spare Core pays culture instead.
Water collector COLLECTOR · 1 WATER · CAP 3
A drop in the collector frame. At token size the frame goes and the glyph grows: on the map the tile colour already says which resource it is sitting on.
Wood collector COLLECTOR · 1 WATER · CAP 3
A two-leaf sprig. Deliberately not a tree: a tree silhouette collapses into a blob at 15 px, and this one keeps its two arms.
Ore collector COLLECTOR · 1 WOOD · CAP 3
A cut lump with one facet line. The facet is what stops it reading as a generic pentagon when the frame is dropped.
Oil collector COLLECTOR · 1 ORE · ONE IN SUPPLY
A derrick, not a second drop. Oil and water are the two the eye confuses first, so they were given different shapes rather than different colours.
Ether collector COLLECTOR · 1 OIL · ONE IN SUPPLY
A four-point star for the only resource that is not a material. There are two ether tiles on every map and one of these in the supply.
Spinner Platform COMBAT · 2 WOOD · 5/2/1
Three blades off a hub, inside a dashed sweep. Defense is 1, not 2 — the open ring is the reminder that it is the fragile one.
Spitter Platform COMBAT · 1 WO 1 OR · 95/1/1
A chassis firing off to the right. Initiative 95 means it acts almost first, and the shots already leaving the barrel are how that reads.
Large Combat COMBAT · 2 WO 1 OR 1 OI · 60/2 AOE/3
The widest chassis on the sheet, with the burst drawn outward. It hits every enemy, so the mark spreads rather than points.
Immobile Combat FORTRESS · 2 WO 2 OR · 98/3/3
Braced to the ground. Never moves, rallies or retreats, so the anchors are the whole point of the drawing and survive to token size.
Wall Platform SHIELD · 3 WOOD · 1/0/4
Courses of brick. It must be shot first, so it is the one mark built to be looked at rather than looked past.
Infiltrator SPY · 1 WA 1 WO 1 OI · NO CORE
The Illusionist’s mask on a circuit stem. Same job, same silhouette, different species — and the only robot unit that needs no Core.
Two decisions this sheet makes on the roster’s behalf. The five collectors drop their frame at token size and keep only the glyph, because on the map the tile colour has already said which resource is underneath. And the two spies share a silhouette across species, which means a player who learns one has learned both — worth more than two unrelated marks that each look clever alone.
