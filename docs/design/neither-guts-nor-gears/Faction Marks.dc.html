Faction marks One mark per faction, drawn on the same 24 px grid at 1.8 px stroke. Three wizard, three robot. Each is shown large, then at the 26 px it has to survive as a map token, on its own faction colour with the dark ring every token carries.
24 PX GRID 1.8 STROKE ROUND CAPS
The Covenant WIZARD
A wand mid-stroke. Already in use on the table strip, so it stays as drawn rather than being redesigned to match the others.
The Foundry ROBOT
A crucible pouring. The one mark that reads as making rather than fighting, which is what separates them from the other two robot seats.
The Unbolted ROBOT
A nut with one side open and the bolt already out of it. The name is the picture, so it needs no reading.
The Ledger ROBOT
An open account book. Fitting for the seat whose whole case is treaty income, and readable even when their Leader is dead.
The Schism WIZARD
The Covenant’s wand, snapped. They broke away over which enemy comes first, so the mark is the same object with a gap in it.
The Brass Circle WIZARD
A toothed ring with a spark inside it. The only mark that carries both species at once, which is exactly their position.

What holds the set together Four rules, and the last one is the one that gets broken.
1 Stroke only, no fill. A filled mark cannot sit on six different faction colours and stay legible on all of them.
2 One silhouette per mark. If two icons share an outline at 26 px, one of them is wrong, whatever the detail inside says.
3 Colour never carries meaning on its own. The mark works in one ink, and the faction colour is added on top of a mark that already reads.
4 Drawn at 26 px first, then scaled up. Every icon here was checked at token size before it was allowed to look good large.
