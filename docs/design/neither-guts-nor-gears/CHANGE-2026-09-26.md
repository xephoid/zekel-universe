# Change note — 26 September 2026

Four new marks, and a drift problem they uncovered. The marks took an hour; the
drift is the reason this file exists.

---

## 1. What was added to the canvas

Four marks in `icons.js`, taking the registry to **85**, and four cards on
`Research Icons.dc.html` (now ten research marks over three rows, frame grown
620 → 760).

| Registry name | Family | Slots | The mark |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Haste` | wizard research | 3 | The `Surf` bowl with a second drop over it |
| `Collector Optimizations` | robot research | 3 | A hex outline with the `Collector` dots twice inside |
| `Core Integration` | robot research | 3 | The `Core` hexagon at 0.9 with an arrow dropping in |
| `Advance Research Station` | **robot building** | 4 | `STATN` + `MAST` + a card with a plus on it |

Call them by those exact strings — `NGG.icon('Haste')` and so on. They return
path data in `d1…d4`, never markup, like every other mark.

Each one quotes something that already exists, which is the registry's rule:
Haste is the unit it upgrades plus the thing the upgrade does; Collector
Optimizations reuses the Collector dots; Core Integration reuses the Core.
Nothing here needs an illustrator.

---

## 2. Advance Research Station is filed as a building, not a research

It arrived described as a robot research option. It *unlocks* research rather
than being research, and it is named Station like the two that already exist, so
I drew it as the third masted station and put it in the buildings family.

**It exists nowhere in the engine** — no catalog entry, no id, no test. It is
the only one of the four that is genuinely unimplemented, and it needs a
decision before it is: a building and a research differ in cost shape, in
prerequisite, and in whether it can be lost. If it lands as a research instead,
the mark should move out of the station family, because three masted stations
that are two buildings and one research would be a lie about the family.

Settle it in `GAME-DESIGN.md` §7.10 first.

## 3. The other three are already in the engine, and the canvas is behind it

This is the part that matters. Rulings **#101–#106** are implemented in
`src/games/neither-guts-nor-gears/` with tests in
`__tests__/rulings101to106.test.ts`, and they carry mechanics no artboard knows
about. **Six boards are now stale.** None of this is an icon problem.

### #101 Haste brought a resource bank with it

`collectorYield()` returns 2 for an untyped collector when the owner has Haste,
and `bankOf()` / `PlayerState.bank` hold surplus production *for the rest of the
round*, spending before collectors on later purchases and emptying at upkeep.

> A bank may pay a purchase with no collectors placed at all — only with Haste.

**`Choice Payment.dc.html` is wrong.** Its whole premise is that a purchase is
paid by placing collectors, and its footer says surplus is simply wasted
("a Surf on a tile you do not need still reaches further, and is still spent").
With Haste there is a second source of payment that is not on the map, and a
payment can be legal with an empty board. That is a new region on the screen,
not a copy fix.

`Robot Payment.dc.html` is unaffected — typed collectors still yield one.

### #102 Collector Optimizations caps at two, and is contagious

`stackAllowed()`: a location holds one collector unless the placer has the
research, **two at most, never more**. And a partner's collector may share only
if *that partner also has the research* and is an anchor partner.

**Eight boards state one-collector-to-a-tile as an invariant**, and the docking
position at the bottom of the hex was designed on the assumption that there is
only ever one. `Choice Payment`'s "a tile that already has one is never lit" is
now conditional on the viewer's research, and on the tile owner's.

The Economic spend still needs its full count of **distinct** tiles, so
`The End.dc.html` survives untouched. Worth saying out loud, because it is the
one place the exception does not apply.

### #103 Core Integration changes a number on a live board

A Core bought *with* a platform is free; a bare Core still costs 1 water.

**`Robot Build.dc.html` prices this the old way** — "fit a spare = income 8→7,
or buy for 1 water". For a seat with the research the second option is free, and
the choice the board is built around mostly evaporates.

### #104 Motherboard and Greymore give +3, not +1

**`Hero Claim.dc.html` and `Setup Draft.dc.html` both print "+1 technology
point while alive and placed"**, and `Smyth Reward.dc.html`'s rail says they
"add a point". Three boards, one number, and it changes what a technology win
costs — 22 unique types with a +3 hero is a different race.

### #105 Envoy Sable Marrow now stops spies

In a battle Envoy is in, no spy — Invisible or Spy Attack — can kill a hero on
Envoy's side.

**`Robot Infiltrator.dc.html` offers Spy Attack with Decoy and Cleric as the
only stated defences.** Envoy is a third and it is absolute. `Spy Reveal` and
`Battle Defense` have the same gap.

### #106 Dowser Wren Calloway has no overlay any more

She now grants one free collector when placed — a Surf for a wizard, or a
collector with a Core already in it for a robot. The catalog says it plainly:
*"No resource overlay."*

**`Overlay Choice.dc.html` is invalid at its premise.** The board is Dowser's
Water overlay against Prospector's Ore on one tile, and Dowser no longer has
one. Three heroes still do — Prospector (either species), Derrick (robots),
Mayhew (wizards) — so the screen is still needed, with a different cast.

She is also still `category: 'economy'` with no overlay, which is worth a look:
the Economy category and the overlay mechanic were the same thing until now.

## 4. What to do, in order

1. **Decide Advance Research Station**, building or research. Everything else
   about it waits on that.
2. **Recast `Overlay Choice.dc.html`** — Prospector against Derrick or Mayhew.
   Cheapest fix with the largest correctness gain, since the board is currently
   describing a rule that no longer exists.
3. **Fix the +3** on Hero Claim, Setup Draft and Smyth Reward. One number,
   three boards, mechanical.
4. **Add Envoy** to the defence list on Robot Infiltrator, Spy Reveal and
   Battle Defense.
5. **Reprice the Core** on Robot Build.
6. **Design the bank** into Choice Payment, and the two-to-a-tile exception into
   the payment screens and the token grammar. These two are real design work,
   not edits — the bank needs a region on the screen and stacking breaks a
   drawing convention that eight boards share.

Items 2–5 are corrections. Item 6 is a commission.

## 5. The lesson worth keeping

The canvas had no way of knowing any of this happened. Six rulings landed in the
engine and every screen that depends on them stayed confident and wrong, and it
took drawing four icons to find out.

`DESIGN-ALIGNMENT.md` was a snapshot taken on 23 September. It should be a
check that runs — at minimum, a list of the canvas claims that quote a number or
a rule, with the engine symbol each one reads from, so a ruling that moves a
number can be traced to the boards that print it. The five worth wiring first
are the ones above: `collectorYield`, `stackAllowed`, the paired-Core price,
the bonus-hero tech points, and the hero effect strings in `data/catalogs.ts`.
