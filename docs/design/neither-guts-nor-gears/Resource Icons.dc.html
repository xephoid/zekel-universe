Where this changes a screen: the cost line. A cost used to read Wo×2 Or×1 — two abbreviations a player has to translate before they can compare anything. It now reads as two pictures and two numbers, in the same coloured pill, so scanning nine buildable things for the one you can afford is looking rather than reading. A cost of one drops the number entirely, because the glyph on its own already says one.
The one compromise Oil is a derrick, which is strictly an extraction shape standing in for the substance. Water and oil are the pair the eye confuses first, and two drops do not survive beside each other at 11 px in a cost pill. Colour cannot carry it either, since the pills are already colour-coded and a colour-blind player would be left with nothing.
