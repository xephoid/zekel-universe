Wizard units — seven marks The whole wizard roster. Each icon is shown large, then on the cream token a Covenant piece actually wears. The cream is the hardest ground in the game, so if a mark holds there it holds on the other five.
7 OF 7 TOKEN 26 PX
Surf COLLECTOR · 2 WATER
An open vessel under a drop. Takes any resource, which is why nothing in the mark names one. Dies to any hit.
Subject FACTION BOARD · 1 WA 1 WO
A figure inside a frame, because a Subject never leaves the faction board and so can never be attacked. The frame is the rule.
Evoker CASTER · 2 WO 1 OR
A staff with the orb already firing. The rays point away, so the mark reads as spending rather than holding — a Missile costs mana a shot.
Illusionist SPY · 2 WO 1 OR
A mask with a spark under it. The mask is shared with the robot Infiltrator on purpose: both do the same job, and the tail tells them apart.
Dragon BEAST · 2 WO 2 OR 1 ET
A wing, layered. The only mark on the sheet that loses a line at token size, which is the honest cost of the most detailed unit in the roster.
Enchantress SUPPORT · 1 WA 1 WO 1 ET
Two chevrons rising off a body. The body is what makes it the unit: the same chevrons without it are the Enhance spell, and flipped they are Dehance.
Cleric SUPPORT · 2 WA 1 ET
A shield, not a cross. Five mana stops one death a pass, so the mark is about preventing a loss rather than healing a wound.
Three of these are gated Illusionist needs the Library of Illusion, Dragon needs the Dragon Hoard, Cleric needs the Temple. An unbuildable unit shows its mark at 40 percent with the building named beneath it, never hidden — the same rule the Build screen follows.
