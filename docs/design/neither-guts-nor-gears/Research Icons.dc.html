Research — six wizard, one robot Research never stands on the map, so these marks live on the faction board, in the cost line of a Research action, and beside the effect in a battle log. The second chip on each card is the researched state: a filled ring, the same one the technology track uses.
7 OF 7 NEVER ON THE MAP
Ice Blast 1 WA 1 WO · EVOKER, 3 MANA
An ice star whose lower arm is an arrowhead. Drops one unit 100 initiative, so the mark points down rather than out.
Mana Shield 2 ETHER · 1 MANA A POINT
The Cleric’s shield drawn dashed, with a mana diamond inside. Dashed because it is bought a point at a time and gone when the mana is.
Dehance 1 WA 1 WO · ENCHANTRESS, 2 MANA
Two chevrons falling, and no body. A mark with a body is a unit; the effect on its own is the spell, so Enhance is these same chevrons rising.
Fiery Explosion 1 WO 1 OR · EVOKER, 3 MANA
A flame with rays going every way. Area damage, so nothing in the mark has a direction — the opposite of Ice Blast on purpose.
Detection 1 WA 1 WO · ENCHANTRESS, FREE
An eye over a dashed line — the hidden card it reads. The dashes are the spy’s own language, borrowed to say what this undoes.
Invisible 1 WA 1 WO · SPY HERO, ON REVEAL
A figure drawn entirely in dashes. The only fully dashed mark in the set, which is the whole idea: present, and not there.
Metal Platforms 2 WO 2 OI · ROBOT · AT HOME
A chassis under a plate, with a chevron for the +1. The robots have exactly one research, so this mark has to carry the whole idea alone.
Battle strategy card NOT RESEARCH · DRAWN BLIND
A card whose face is dashed, because you draw it without seeing it. A Research action can buy one instead, so it belongs on this sheet without being research.
Three marks quote a unit on purpose. Mana Shield is the Cleric’s shield gone dashed. Dehance is the Enchantress upside down. Invisible borrows the spy’s dashes. A player who knows the roster can read the research without being taught it twice, and the ones that quote nothing — Ice Blast, Fiery Explosion — are the two that change how a whole battle resolves.
