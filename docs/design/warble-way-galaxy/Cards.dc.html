The small code in each band (♠ A) still names the matching playing card, so a normal deck works until these are printed.

HOW A CARD READS

  1. Band: the kind of card, its icon and colour, and the playing-card code on the right.
  2. Picture: one icon per card, in the kind's colour, inside a dashed scanner ring. Recruits show the race portrait where your drawings exist.
  3. Name: what you met. Hazard names are my suggestions from the icons; rename freely.
  4. Face: exactly the numbers the table printed. Two-way cards (hazards, jobs) give each way its own line.

The five kind colours also differ in lightness, and each has its own icon and word, so colour is never the only clue. Switch the stock from the Tweaks panel to see the whole deck in another colour.
