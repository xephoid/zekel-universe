The picture on a piece tells you which one it is:
Leader · candy crown
Gathers influence. Claims the castle once its monster is gone.
Knight · sword
Fights rival Knights and the castle monster. Captures pieces.
Ambassador · wrapped candy
Draws Intel cards. Travel far from home to draw more.
3
Cards move you by color
Play a red card: one piece jumps forward to the next red space, however far that is. No counting.
Pieces always travel counter-clockwise round the four lands. At each land’s first purple space (the junction) you may turn onto the road that leads to the Candy Castle.
4
Your turn, in four steps
  1. Recall (optional). Send any of your pieces back home.
  2. Draw. Press Draw to take Intel cards: more when your Ambassador is far from home.
  3. Play one card to move a piece and take its color’s action. Or pass for +1 influence.
  4. Spend influence (optional): 1 buys an Intel token, 3 buys a sugar cube. Then End turn.
Sugar cubes are your battle dice. Each one rolls 0, 0, 0, 1, 2 or 3. Battles only happen when you press Roll.
5
Every color is an action
After moving, you may take the card’s action. Each action helps one piece most.
Battle · Knight
Fight a Knight, capture a piece, or attack the castle.
Influence · Leader
Gain influence; more the farther from home.
Taxes · any piece
Rivals in your land pay you. Then raise the tax.
Free play · any piece
Or discard it to play many cards this turn.
Intel · Ambassador
Draw cards; more the farther from home.
Castle · Leader or Knight
From the road, step into the Candy Castle.
