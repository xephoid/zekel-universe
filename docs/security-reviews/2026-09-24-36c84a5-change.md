## Findings

**SEC-16-F01 (fixed): questions had no limit.** Each `query` costs an engine
call and is not serialized behind moves, so one connection could send them
as fast as it liked. Fix: at most 30 per connection in any 10 seconds; the
rest get `rate_limited` without reaching the engine. A person placing
collectors asks once per placement. Test: 35 rapid questions, 30 answered,
the rest refused, 30 engine calls.
