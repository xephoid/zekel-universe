## Findings

### SEC-09-F04 — Burning the safehouse relocated the Hacker without asking (fixed)

- Severity and confidence: **high** for a game whose rules give this decision
  to the player; confirmed from the user's own play and reproduced in tests.
- Affected entry point and source link: the sibling engine's
  `applyBurnSafehouse`, reached through `POST /api/tables/:id/move`.
- Actor, preconditions, and violated boundary: any digital human Hacker.
  The engine-side secret pick was keyed on both seats being *tracked*, and
  digital mode makes a human seat tracked — so a digital human pair took the
  all-AI path. Where the Hacker hides is theirs to choose, at setup and at a
  burn alike. This is the third instance of the same conflation, after the
  setup hideout and the Detective's informants.
- Expected versus observed behavior: expected a chooser. Observed the hideout
  changing from Shizuoka Megamall to Trailer Towers with no prompt.
- Minimal synthetic reproduction and side-effect assertions: burn as a digital
  human Hacker and read `pending` and `hideout_location_id` — the location had
  already changed and nothing was pending.
- Impact and affected milestones: a decision the printed rules give to the
  player was made by the server. No information leaked; the engine keeps the
  hideout out of the Detective's view either way.
- Proposed fix and regression test: fixed in `acbb433` — the pick is keyed on
  whether the AI plays the Hacker. Regression tests in `digitalMode.test.ts`.
- Status, owner, next action, and review date: fixed and verified live.

### SEC-09-F05 — A map tap spent the mid-game guess with no confirmation (fixed)

- Severity and confidence: medium; confirmed from the user's play and live.
- Affected entry point and source link:
  [`pick` in the table page](../../apps/web/src/pages/Table.tsx).
- Actor, preconditions, and violated boundary: the Detective, after the
  turn's mandatory Location play. A tap resolving to one listed move was sent
  immediately, and by then the only move naming a region was the guess.
- Expected versus observed behavior: expected to be told what the tap would
  cost. Observed four action points and the once-per-game guess spent on a tap
  that had meant something harmless a moment earlier.
- Minimal synthetic reproduction and side-effect assertions: covered in
  `glue.test.ts`; live, the sheet now appears and Escape leaves AP at 4.
- Impact and affected milestones: a player could lose their one mid-game guess
  by accident. Nothing was exposed and nothing illegal could be sent.
- Proposed fix and regression test: fixed here.
- Status, owner, next action, and review date: fixed and verified live.
