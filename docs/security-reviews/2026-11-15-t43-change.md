## What changed

- Presentation only. On a phone the Adventurer Olympics screen moves the
  side column and your board into a sheet, the panels over the map into
  bottom sheets, and the legend behind a Key button.
- Universe: a glue may ask for the last move in the phone menu
  (`captionInPhoneMenu`); the map may keep a hex at a minimum size
  (`minRadius`). Both are optional and change nothing when absent.
- Universe: the shared `Sheet`'s focus trap and its first focus skip controls
  taken out of the Tab order (`tabindex="-1"`). Before, with the adventurer
  sheet's roving tabs, Tab could leave the modal and Shift+Tab could land on
  a tab that is not selected; focus could also open on such a tab. Every
  sheet in every game uses this trap; a sheet without such controls behaves
  as before.
- No server, socket, engine-client, database, dependency, build or hosting
  change, and no new request, route or stored data. One Docker-related line:
  zekel-games' `docker-build-files.txt` gains a Sweetlands file its `main`
  already ships (added there by #21 without the list being regenerated). The
  list names files copied into the image; the line adds no file that was not
  already tracked, and `tests/dockerBuildFiles.test.ts` checks the list
  matches the tracked files.
- Universe (after Codex's third pass): in the motion root, a part marked
  `data-flip-ghost` that moves under an id already on the table now flies as
  a copy over the table, as a card does, instead of sliding inside its own
  box, where a scrolling sheet could clip it. It changes only how such marks
  move: Adventurer Olympics' kept-card pile, Cybernoir's station diamonds
  and Sweetlands' discard top. `motion.test.tsx` covers it.
- After Codex's review (2026-11-15): a watcher with no seat can open
  "Players and quests" (Everyone and Quests) on a phone, and the top of every
  bottom sheet carries the opener, so a test, exploring or "Any rune" never
  hides that public information. Both only open the sheet; they send nothing
  (`ao-phone.test.tsx`). The sheet shows only what the desktop side column
  shows the same viewer.
