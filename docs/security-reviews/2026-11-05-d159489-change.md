## What changed

No route, socket event, cookie, storage or dependency changed in Universe.
The screen reads more fields from the view and from each listed move (a
`preview`), draws them as text, and sends the same listed moves as before.
The setup form lets an ability score be left blank. The engine publishes the
new fields only to a seat that plays on a screen.

  `fixtures/ww-plain/` and tested), so Universe can ship before the engine
  change is live without drawing a wrong number.
- The numbers on screen (chances, what a roll adds, what a hit costs) are the
  engine's. Universe formats them and works out none.
