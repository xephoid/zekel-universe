## Findings

### SEC-09-F02 — The Hacker's hand was never lit (fixed here)

- Severity and confidence: low; confirmed on a live table and in fixtures.
- Affected entry point and source link: `litParts` in
  [the Cybernoir glue](../../apps/web/src/glue/cybernoir-2127.ts).
- Actor, preconditions, and violated boundary: any Hacker seat. Cybernoir's
  play moves name a person (`person_name`, `people`); `litParts` only lit hand
  cards by a `hand_index` field, which is a different game's move shape. So no
  card in the Hacker's hand ever lit, however many the engine was offering.
- Expected versus observed behavior: expected every card the engine lists a
  move for to light. Observed a dark hand throughout.
- Minimal synthetic reproduction and side-effect assertions: a Hacker view with
  a hand and a `play_person` naming one of its cards — `litParts` returned
  nothing for it. No side effects: this withheld moves, it never sent one.
- Impact and affected milestones: the numbered menu still listed every move, so
  nothing was unreachable; the table simply did not show what was playable.
  Nothing was exposed and nothing extra could be submitted.
- Proposed fix and regression test: fixed here — cards are lit by the names a
  move carries, and a tap on one means every move naming it. Covered in
  `glue.test.ts`, and it is what makes item 8's "a stack holding a legal card
  lights itself" possible at all.
- Status, owner, next action, and review date: fixed and verified.
