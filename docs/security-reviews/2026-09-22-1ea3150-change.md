## Findings

### SEC-09-F03 — The Detective's hand was not lit either (fixed here)

- Severity and confidence: low; confirmed on a live table and in fixtures.
- Affected entry point and source link: `litParts` in
  [the Cybernoir glue](../../apps/web/src/glue/cybernoir-2127.ts).
- Actor, preconditions, and violated boundary: any Detective seat. The same
  class as [SEC-09-F02](2026-09-22-a68934a-change.md) in the other seat: a
  Location move names a location, and `litParts` lit the **map region** with
  that name but never the card in hand holding it. So a Location could be
  played by tapping the city and not by tapping the card.
- Expected versus observed behavior: expected the card the engine is offering
  to light. Observed a dark hand beside a lit city.
- Minimal synthetic reproduction and side-effect assertions: a Detective view
  with `location_hand` and a `play_location` naming one of its cards —
  `litParts` returned the region id and no hand-card id. No side effects: this
  withheld a route to a move, it never sent one.
- Impact and affected milestones: nothing was unreachable, since the region and
  the numbered menu both worked. Nothing was exposed.
- Proposed fix and regression test: fixed here — a card lights for every move
  that names what is on it, people and locations alike, and a tap on it means
  those moves. Covered in `glue.test.ts`.
- Status, owner, next action, and review date: fixed and verified.
