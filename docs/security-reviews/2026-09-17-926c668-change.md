## Findings

### SEC-13-F06 — A database that failed to migrate stayed open

- Severity and confidence: P3, reproduced (the new migration test could not delete its temporary file because the refused connection kept it open).
- Affected entry point and source link: `createDatabase` in [apps/server/src/db/index.ts](../../apps/server/src/db/index.ts).
- Actor, preconditions, and violated boundary: operational only. A refused start (schema mismatch) left a SQLite handle or a Postgres pool open until the process exited.
- Expected versus observed behavior: the connection closed on the way out; observed left open.
- Minimal synthetic reproduction and side-effect assertions: `db.test.ts` "upgrades a version 2 database in place and refuses a newer one".
- Impact and affected milestones: none beyond tidiness; a refused start exits anyway.
- Proposed fix and regression test: done; `migrateOrClose` destroys the connection before rethrowing.
- Status, owner, next action, and review date: fixed 2026-09-17.

### SEC-15-F07 — A nudge email discloses the game to the inbox

- Severity and confidence: P3, source-confirmed.
- Affected entry point and source link: `sendDueNudges` in [apps/server/src/realtime.ts](../../apps/server/src/realtime.ts).
- Actor, preconditions, and violated boundary: whoever reads the recipient's inbox learns they play that game at that time. The recipient chose email sign-in and a by-turns table, so this is within what they asked for, but the brief has no decision on it.
- Expected versus observed behavior: a decision recorded in the brief on what the nudge may say; observed the game name in the subject and body.
- Minimal synthetic reproduction and side-effect assertions: not applicable (content).
- Impact and affected milestones: privacy expectation (M3).
- Proposed fix and regression test: decide in the brief; if the name must go, the subject becomes "Your move" and the body keeps only the link.
- Status, owner, next action, and review date: open; owner: product; next action: a line in the brief; review at the next full review.
