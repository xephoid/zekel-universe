## Findings

### SEC-09-F01 — The engine chooses the Hacker's hideout for them

- Severity and confidence: medium; confirmed by running the engine directly.
- Affected entry point and source link: not this repository. The sibling
  engine's `createInitialState` for Cybernoir 2127, reached through
  `POST /api/tables` → `tables/service.ts` `startTable` → `create_session`.
- Actor, preconditions, and violated boundary: any Cybernoir table opened from
  Universe. Universe marks human seats `digital`, which the engine reads as
  *tracked*; the engine auto-completes setup whenever both seats are tracked,
  so it picks the hideout and returns a session already in the play phase. The
  boundary is the project's own rule that a decision the printed rules give to
  a player is never made by the server. The engine's own setup checklist
  carries the wording for a human Hacker choosing, so this contradicts its
  intent as well.
- Expected versus observed behavior: expected a session in the setup phase with
  one legal move for the Hacker, `report_hideout` with a blank location name.
  Observed the play phase and a hideout already set, in all three digital
  seatings (human Hacker against an AI Detective, human Detective against an AI
  Hacker, and two humans).
- Minimal synthetic reproduction and side-effect assertions: create a session
  with two `table: 'digital'` players and read `phase` and
  `hacker.hideout_location_id`. No side effects beyond an in-memory session.
- Impact and affected milestones: the Hacker's one secret decision is taken
  from them. No information leaks — the engine keeps the hideout out of every
  Detective-facing view either way — so this is an agency fault, not a
  disclosure one.
- Proposed fix and regression test: in the engine, auto-complete setup only
  when the Hacker seat is an **AI**, separating who holds the cards from who
  decides. Regression: a digital human Hacker starts in the setup phase with a
  `report_hideout` that needs a name.
- Status, owner, next action, and review date: open, owned by the engine
  repository. Universe's side is built and tested and does nothing until the
  engine stops auto-picking. Recheck when the engine change lands, by running
  `e2e/cybernoir-two-browser.spec.ts`, which now picks the hideout when asked.
