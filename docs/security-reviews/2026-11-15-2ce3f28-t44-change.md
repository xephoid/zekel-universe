## Findings

None in the tested scope.

Outside this change, in zekel-games main (not a security issue, a rules
one): Sweetlands' legal moves list "Play green: move <piece> and take the
green action" for each green card, but the engine plays any green move
with `takeAction` as "discard for unlimited plays, no move". The player is
told a piece will move and it does not, on the desktop too. It needs its
own fix in the engine; the phone spec avoids green cards meanwhile.
